title: Strip GPS location
desc: Removes the GPS location (EXIF, XMP, QuickTime) from the images and videos uploaded to the forum (attachments, personal files, avatars) before they are stored. An upload whose location cannot be removed is refused. Requires exiftool (/usr/bin/exiftool or /usr/local/bin/exiftool, or the path in $PHORUM['mod_strip_image_gps']['exiftool']).
version: 1.0.0
author: LibreForum
category: admin

hook: file_store|phorum_mod_strip_image_gps_file_store
priority: run hook file_store before store_files_on_disk
